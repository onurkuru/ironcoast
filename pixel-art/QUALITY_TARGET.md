# Cinematic pixel-art quality target

The user supplied a REPLACED scene on 17 September 2026 as a quality benchmark.
Use its craft and hierarchy as reference for the original Iron Coast setting.
This is not approval of the current game's visual quality or permission to claim
equivalent animation, lighting technology or production value.

- Adult silhouettes must read before background detail. Keep the floor behind
  feet quiet enough to establish contact in motion.
- Concentrate contrast around motivated practical lights and points of action.
  Most wall detail belongs in grouped dark values, with controlled pixel clusters.
- Use restrained warm lamps against cool ambient light; avoid outlines and
  uniformly bright texture on every object.
- Separate foreground occlusion, playable architecture and distant atmosphere.
  A large background image alone does not meet this target.
- Show material-specific floor reflections, contact shadows and restrained light
  scattering. Any overlay must stay attached to the same scene geometry.
- Keep actor and environment pixel density coherent; do not substitute a global
  pixelation filter or palette reduction for authored shapes and animation.
- Review actual walk, climb, attack, hit and camera transitions at output size.
  A generated still, passing tests or a successful build is not artistic approval.

The first integrated Marsh painting established route layout. The second pass
refined lighting, contrast hierarchy and detail density, and the playable route
was checked with the actual hero, combat and camera. Remaining work includes
independent background motion, richer actor lighting and animation transitions;
the current pass is a development milestone, not final artistic approval.
